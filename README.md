# Bitcoin Price Forecasting & Data Analysis

**Academic Research Portfolio | Muhammad Adjie Daud**

## Project Overview

This project documents academic research and data analysis related to Bitcoin (BTC) historical prices and time-series forecasting.

The project focuses on understanding historical price behavior, preparing financial data for analysis, exploring forecasting methods, and presenting research findings through structured documentation, tables, and visualizations.

This repository serves as a portfolio of work in data analytics, time-series analysis, and artificial intelligence and machine learning.

## Objectives

- Organize and examine historical Bitcoin price data.
- Understand data quality and preprocessing requirements.
- Explore historical price patterns through descriptive statistics and visualization.
- Document the forecasting methodology used in the research.
- Evaluate forecasting performance using appropriate error metrics, where available.
- Communicate findings, limitations, and potential improvements transparently.

## Research Scope

### 1. Data Collection
Document the source, observation period, data fields, and collection procedure for the historical Bitcoin dataset.

### 2. Data Preparation
Describe data quality checks, missing-value handling, duplicate checks, and any preprocessing steps actually performed.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis
Present historical price visualizations, descriptive statistics, and relevant observations from the dataset.

### 4. Forecasting Methodology
Document the forecasting model or models used in the final research, including the modeling procedure and relevant assumptions.

### 5. Model Evaluation
Include actual evaluation results and appropriate metrics, such as Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), or Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE), when supported by the research.

### 6. Results and Limitations
Present verified findings, forecast visualizations, research limitations, and considerations when interpreting future price estimates.
